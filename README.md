# TP03_C2_Gerry_ShootingAtThings

> [!TIP]
> ### Controls
> | KEY     | FUNCTIONALITY |
> | ---      | ---       |
> | WASD | Move |
> | SPACE | Jump |
> | Left Ctrl | Crouch |
> | Left Shift | Walk |
> | MOUSE CURSOR | Look Around |
> | Left Click | Shoot |
> | Mouse Scrollwheel / Numbers (1-2) | Change Weapon |
